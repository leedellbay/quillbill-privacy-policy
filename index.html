Privacy Policy

Quillbill — private, offline invoicing for one-person businesses

Published by Lee Dell Bay Innovation · Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Quillbill (app ID com.leedellbay.quillbill), published by Lee Dell Bay Innovation (“we”, “us”).

The app itself sends nothing to us. Your business details, clients, invoices and payments stay on your device. There is no account and no server of ours. The only network traffic the app generates is from Google’s advertising software, described in section 3.

There are two separate things going on, and this policy keeps them apart: what the app does with your information, and what Google’s advertising software inside the app does. Only the second involves anybody outside your device.

1. Information the app collects

Quillbill stores what you type so it can work the way you left it:

  • Your business details (name, address, email, phone, numbering and tax preferences).
  • Your company logo, if you choose one: a resized copy of the single image you pick, kept inside the app. The app receives only the file you select and cannot browse your photo library.
  • Your social media handles and any other links you add, which print in the invoice footer.
  • Your clients (name, and optionally email and address).
  • Your invoices, line items and recorded payments.
  • Display preferences (theme, colour, text size, default invoice-list columns).

All of it is stored on your device, in the app’s private storage. None of it is sent to us. There is no user account, sign-in, password or profile on any server. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone, and has no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no in-app purchases.

2. When information leaves your device because you chose it

Quillbill can create a PDF of an invoice (which includes your logo and social links if you set them), or a spreadsheet or PDF report of payments received. Invoice PDFs are written to a temporary file and handed to your phone’s share sheet. “Email to client” instead opens your own mail app, addressed to the client with the PDF attached, using the opening and signature you set; the app never sends the email itself, you do (if the device cannot do this, the PDF goes to the share sheet). Payment reports, and invoice PDFs you choose to save, are written to the folder you pick in your phone’s own “save as” screen; Quillbill keeps no copy, and they are yours to share or delete from there (“Erase all data” does not delete files you saved). If you add payment links (such as PayPal or Stripe), they are stored on your device and printed on your invoices; Quillbill does not process payments and never sees what a client pays through them. Where they go next — email, messages, cloud storage, another app — is decided by you and governed by that app’s own terms. We never receive them.

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Google’s advertising software runs inside the app and collects information automatically. This happens even though the app itself sends us nothing.

The advertising software has no access to the information described in section 1. It cannot read your clients, invoices or payments.

4. Advertising and personalised advertising

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • iPhone and iPad. The app shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, ads are non-personalised and the whole app still works.
  • EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland. The app shows Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. If you refuse, you still see ads, but non-personalised ones.
  • Elsewhere, ads may be personalised by default. You can change this at adssettings.google.com, or reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Privacy → Ads settings.
  • In the app, Settings → Privacy links to these controls and reopens the consent form where one applies.

The advertising ID is “Data Used to Track You” on the App Store. The macOS version of Quillbill shows no ads and collects nothing.

5. How long information is kept, and deleting it

Everything the app stores stays on your device until you delete it. Settings → Erase all data removes your business details, logo, clients, invoices and payments at once, and uninstalling the app removes everything. Because we hold none of it, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Google keeps what it receives under its own policy.

6. Information about your clients

If you store personal information about your own customers, you decide what is kept and for how long. It never reaches us, so requests from your customers to see or delete their details are for you to handle. You can edit or delete any client, invoice or payment at any time.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s own security (screen lock and, on most devices, storage encryption). We do not operate a server that could be breached. Anyone who can unlock your device can open the app, so keep your device locked.

8. Children

Quillbill is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age of consent in some countries). It does not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal information from the app, we will point you to the right place at Google for advertising data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above and say so in the release notes.
